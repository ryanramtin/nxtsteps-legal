Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains exactly what data NxtSteps AI collects, why we collect it, and how we protect it. We will never sell your personal information.

1. Who We Are

NxtSteps AI ("we," "our," or "us") is an iOS application developed by Ryan Ramtin (ryanramtin@gmail.com). The app's bundle identifier is ai.nxtsteps.mobile. Our registered address and governing jurisdiction is the State of California, United States.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide and improve the NxtSteps AI service:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile picture when you sign up (via Apple Sign-In or email).
  • Audio & Transcription Data: Microphone audio captured during meetings and voice memos you choose to record. Audio is processed to generate text transcripts.
  • Meeting Content: Transcripts, AI-generated summaries, extracted action items, risks, blockers, and decisions produced from your recordings.
  • Usage Data: App interactions, feature usage, crash reports, and performance metrics collected to improve the app.
  • Device & Technical Data: Device model, OS version, push notification token, and IP address for authentication and notifications.
  • Location Data (optional): Approximate location used solely to determine local sunrise/sunset times for automatic light/dark theme switching. Location is never stored on our servers.
  • Photos/Camera (optional): Profile pictures you choose to upload or capture. Images are stored securely and only associated with your account.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data to:

  • Transcribe your meetings and generate AI-powered insights (action items, risks, decisions).
  • Sync your data across your devices under your account.
  • Send push notifications about completed processing or upcoming deadlines.
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your workspace with Face ID / Touch ID.
  • Diagnose bugs, improve performance, and develop new features.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate service-related updates.

We do not use your meeting content or transcripts to train any AI models, and we do not share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. AI Processing & Third-Party AI Providers

NxtSteps AI uses large-language model (LLM) APIs to generate meeting summaries and extract signals. Your transcript data may be transmitted to the following AI API providers for processing:

  • Anthropic, Inc. (Claude API) – Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI, LLC (OpenAI API, where applicable) – Privacy Policy

Both providers process data under their own privacy policies. We transmit only the transcript content necessary for AI processing — no personally identifiable account information is sent alongside transcript data unless required for context. Neither provider is permitted to use your data submitted via API to train their models.

5. On-Device Processing

Where possible, NxtSteps AI performs speech recognition on-device using Apple's Speech Recognition framework. On-device processing means your audio never leaves your device for that stage of transcription. A network connection is only required when syncing results to your account or when cloud AI processing is invoked.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data and meeting records for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will permanently delete your personal data and meeting content within 30 days, unless we are required to retain it by law. Aggregate, de-identified analytics data may be retained indefinitely.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) for all data sent between the app and our servers.
  • Encryption at rest for stored meeting transcripts and personal data.
  • Biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID) to lock access to your workspace on-device.
  • Tenant-isolated data storage to prevent cross-user data access.

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong passcode and keep your device's OS up to date.

8. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • AI API Providers: As described in Section 4, transcript data is sent to Anthropic and/or OpenAI for processing.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Cloud hosting, database, and CDN providers that store and transmit data on our behalf under data processing agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or government authority, we may disclose data to comply with legal obligations.
  • Business Transfers: If NxtSteps AI is acquired or merged, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your data is subject to a different privacy policy.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Portability: Request a machine-readable export of your data.
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ryanramtin@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Children's Privacy

NxtSteps AI is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know, delete, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To submit a request or designate an authorized agent, contact us at ryanramtin@gmail.com.

We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, by sending an in-app notification. Continued use of NxtSteps AI after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
